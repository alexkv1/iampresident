Toast notification — confirmations ("Farm started.") and errors ("Not enough resources.").

```jsx
<Toast kind="ok">You are now Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson of Sweden</Toast>
<Toast kind="err">Not enough resources.</Toast>
<Toast kind="ok" icon="trophy">Mark Carney has won the game!</Toast>
```

- Stack in a fixed column at top: 16px, centred, gap 8px. Auto-dismiss after 2600ms with a 250ms fade.
